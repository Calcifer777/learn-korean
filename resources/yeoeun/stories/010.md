야근 (Overtime Work)

오늘은 할 일이 너무 많았어요. 그래서 야근을 했어요.
I had too much work to do today. So I did overtime work.

보고서 마감이 내일까지잖아요.
The report deadline is until tomorrow, you know.

저녁 8시쯤에 배가 고프기 시작했어요.
Around 8 PM, I started getting hungry.

동료들과 피자를 시켜 먹었어요. 피자를 먹으면서 일했어요.
I ordered and ate pizza with my colleagues. We worked while eating pizza.

"우리 언제 퇴근할까요?" 동료가 물었어요.
"When shall we leave work?" a colleague asked.

"10시까지는 끝냅시다." 제가 말했어요.
"Let's finish by 10 o'clock," I said.

야근은 정말 피곤해요. 하지만 끝내야 하는 일이니까 열심히 했어요.
Overtime is really tiring. But since it's work that must be finished, I worked hard.

드디어 10시에 일이 끝났어요.
Finally, at 10 o'clock, the work finished.

우리는 택시를 타고 집으로 갔어요. 내일은 주말이니까 푹 쉴 거예요.
We took a taxi and went home. Since tomorrow is the weekend, I will rest deeply.

---

할 일, Work to do
야근, Overtime work
마감, Deadline
내일, Tomorrow
시켜 먹다, To order and eat (food delivery)
퇴근하다, To leave work (go home)
-(으)ㅂ시다, Let's (Formal)
피곤하다, To be tired
끝내다, To finish
드디어, Finally
택시, Taxi
주말, Weekend
푹, Deeply / Fully (rest/sleep)
쉬다, To rest
