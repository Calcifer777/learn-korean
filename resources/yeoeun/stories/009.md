회사 회의 (Office Meeting)

오늘 오전 10시에 회의가 있었어요.
There was a meeting at 10 AM today.

부장님께서 "이번 프로젝트는 아주 중요합니다."라고 말씀하셨어요.
The department head said, "This project is very important."

저는 지난주부터 준비한 보고서를 발표했어요.
I presented the report I had prepared since last week.

발표를 하기 전에 정말 떨렸어요.
I was really nervous before doing the presentation.

하지만 동료들이 저를 응원해 주었어요.
But my colleagues cheered for me.

"마르코 씨, 잘할 수 있어요!"라고 말했어요.
They said, "Marco, you can do well!"

발표가 끝난 후에 부장님께서 칭찬해 주셨어요.
After the presentation finished, the department head praised me.

"수고했어요. 아주 좋은 아이디어예요."
"Good work. It is a very good idea."

회의실 분위기가 밝아졌어요. 저는 안도했어요.
The atmosphere in the meeting room became bright. I was relieved.

---

부장님, Department head (Boss)

칭찬하다, To praise
수고하다, To work hard / take trouble
아이디어, Idea
회의실, Meeting room

밝아지다, To become bright
