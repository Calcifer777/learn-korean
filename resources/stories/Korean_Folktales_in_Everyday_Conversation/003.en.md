The Story of the Green Frog

In a small pond, there lived a mother green frog and a son green frog.
The son green frog did not listen to his mother.
He did everything the exact opposite way and broke his mother's heart.

"My child, please don't play in dirty places. Play in clean places."
Whenever the mother frog said this, the son frog would purposely swim and play wildly in messy, muddy water.

"My child, there are many snakes in the forest, so don't go there. It's dangerous."
Whenever the mother frog said this, the son frog would go out of his way to play in the forest.
There was more than one occasion where he was almost eaten by a snake.

"My child, try crying 'gaegul-gaegul' (ribbit-ribbit)."
The mother frog taught him how to croak.
However, doing the opposite of what his mother said, the son frog cried 'gulgae-gulgae'.

Like this, the son green frog always did the exact opposite of what his mother told him.
The mother frog was really upset because of her son.
"Sigh, what should I do with him not listening like that?"

Then one day, the mother green frog caught a severe illness.
She knew she would pass away soon.
So she called her son and said:

"When I die, make sure to bury me by the stream. You must never bury me in the mountains."
The mother frog actually wanted to be buried in the mountains.
But she was worried that if she asked to be buried in the mountains, her son would bury her by the stream instead.
So she purposely asked to be buried by the stream instead of the mountains.
She thought her son would then do the opposite and bury her in the mountains.

A little while later, the mother frog passed away.
When his mother died, the son frog shed buckets of tears.
"Mom caught a terrible illness because I didn't listen to her. Mom died because of me. Sob sob."

The son frog deeply regretted not listening to his mother.
And the son frog thought to himself:
"I must absolutely listen to the very last words my mom left behind."

The son frog buried the mother frog by the stream, exactly as she had told him to do.
One day after burying his mother, it started to rain heavily.
The son frog suddenly started worrying about his mother's grave.
"What if mom's grave washes away in the rain? This won't do. I have to go and protect her grave."

When he went to the stream, he saw that his mother's grave really was about to be washed away.
The son frog cried loudly out of worry that the grave would be swept away by the water.

Ever since that day, whenever it rains, the son frog goes to his mother's grave.
And he guards the grave while crying 'gaegul-gaegul'.
That is why, even today, it is said that green frogs by the stream croak loudly whenever it rains.
