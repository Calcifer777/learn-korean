Heungbu and Nolbu

Once upon a time, in a certain village, lived two brothers named Heungbu and Nolbu. The younger brother, Heungbu, was kindhearted, but the older brother, Nolbu, was very greedy. One day, Heungbu and Nolbu's father passed away. Before he died, their father said to them, "Nolbu, Heungbu, you must get along well with each other. Be sure to divide the inheritance between the two of you". However, the greedy Nolbu did not listen to his father's words and kept all the wealth for himself. He then kicked Heungbu's family entirely out of the house.

Heungbu's family ended up living in a small hut they built at the foot of a mountain. Heungbu tried to find food for his family, but it was difficult to get anything to eat because it was winter. "Father, I'm hungry. There is nothing to eat in the house, Father," his children cried. Seeing his children unable to eat anything broke Heungbu's heart. "I can't just sit here, dear. I will go visit my older brother's house today," he told his wife. Having no other choice, Heungbu went to see Nolbu. "Brother, my children are starving. Please, just give me a little bit of rice," he pleaded. "You rascal, where do you think you are?" yelled the greedy Nolbu as he ran over with a broom and hit Heungbu. Nolbu's greedy wife also grabbed a rice scoop and slapped Heungbu's cheek with it.

When she did that, the grains of rice that had been stuck to the scoop clung to Heungbu's cheek. Heungbu was so hungry that he ate the rice grains off his face. Wanting to get more rice grains for his children, Heungbu offered his other cheek as well. "Sister-in-law, please hit this cheek with the scoop too," he asked. "What? Get out right now!" she shouted. In the end, Heungbu had to return home without getting any more rice grains.

Time passed, and spring arrived. A pair of swallows built a nest under the roof of Heungbu's house and laid eggs. Soon, baby swallows were born. One day, a baby swallow fell from the nest and injured its leg. Heungbu carefully treated the baby swallow's leg. Thanks to the kind Heungbu, the baby swallow grew up healthy. When autumn came, the swallow family prepared to leave Heungbu's house for a warmer place. "Have a safe trip, and be sure to come back again next year," Heungbu said as he watched the departing swallow family.

The following spring, the swallow family returned to Heungbu's house. But one swallow circled round and round above Heungbu's head and dropped something it had been holding in its beak. It was a gourd seed. Seeing this, Heungbu's son said, "Father, it's the swallow you treated last year. The swallow brought a gourd seed. It must be a gift for us". Heungbu planted the gourd seed gifted by the swallow in the ground and grew it with care. When autumn arrived, huge gourds hung from the vines in clusters.

Heungbu and his wife picked one of the gourds and started sawing it open. When it was cut about halfway, the gourd split open with a loud "pop" sound, and rice poured out from inside. Heungbu was startled. "Goodness, why is there rice in here?" he wondered. Heungbu and his wife were overjoyed. They quickly sawed open another gourd. This time, with a "pop" sound, treasures poured out from inside the gourd. "Oh my goodness, dear, hurry up and open the other gourds," she said. From the next gourd, a huge house emerged. And from the gourd after that, countless servants came out and bowed to Heungbu. In just one day, Heungbu became the richest man in the village.

Hearing the news about Heungbu, Nolbu came to visit him. Nolbu was incredibly envious and jealous that Heungbu had become wealthy. He asked Heungbu how he had become so rich. The kindhearted Heungbu told Nolbu everything that had happened up to that point. After hearing Heungbu's story, Nolbu went up to the mountain and searched everywhere for a swallow. He finally spotted one. The greedy Nolbu intentionally broke the swallow's leg. Then, he treated the leg and said, "I have treated your leg, so you must absolutely bring me a gourd seed next spring".

The following spring, the swallow actually came to visit Nolbu and gave him a gourd seed. Excited, Nolbu planted the seed and grew it eagerly. When autumn came, gourds hung in clusters at Nolbu's house as well. "Now I am going to be the richest man in the world," he boasted. Nolbu and his wife started sawing open a gourd. When they had cut it about halfway, the gourd split open with a "pop" sound. However, what came out of the gourd were not treasures, but terrifying goblins. The goblins punished the wicked Nolbu and destroyed his house. Nolbu became a beggar in a single day.

"I guess I'm being punished for being so mean to my little brother," Nolbu lamented. "You can say that again. What do we do now?" his wife cried. Nolbu and his wife reflected on their cruel behavior toward Heungbu. Right then, the kind Heungbu, who had heard the news about his brother, came running to Nolbu's ruined home. Heungbu spoke to the ruined Nolbu, saying, "Brother, please don't worry. From now on, come live with our family". "Little brother, I am truly sorry. I was wrong," Nolbu sincerely apologized to Heungbu. The kind Heungbu forgave Nolbu and brought Nolbu's family back to his home. And so, Heungbu and Nolbu lived together happily and harmoniously.
