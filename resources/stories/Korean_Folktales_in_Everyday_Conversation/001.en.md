This is such a classic Korean tale. It’s the perfect example of a "comedy of errors" where two characters are terrified of things that don't actually exist!

Here is the full translation for you:
The Tiger and the Dried Persimmon

It was a cold winter night. A hungry tiger came down to a village in search of food.

"Roar, I’m so hungry."

Just then, he heard a child crying.

"Wah! Wah!"

The tiger began to look for the house where the sound was coming from.

"Oho, it’s that house!"

The tiger arrived at a house where a mother and child were staying. He quietly approached the front door.

"Wah! Wah!"

The child was still crying loudly.

"Child, stop crying. If you keep crying, a goblin (dokkaebi) will come and take you away."

At the mother's words, the child cried even louder. "Wah!"

Hearing the child’s crying, the tiger thought to himself:

"He's not afraid of a goblin? Well, it takes something like a tiger to be truly scary."

Just then, the mother said to the child:

"Child, stop crying. There is a scary tiger outside. If you keep crying, the tiger will take you away."

"Wh-what? How did she know I was here?"

The tiger was startled. Yet, the child still did not stop crying.

"Wah!"

"That kid isn't even afraid of me," the tiger thought, feeling flustered. The mother spoke to the child again:

"Child, look. Mommy will give you a dried persimmon (gotgam). A dried persimmon!"

At the word "gotgam," the child stopped crying instantly.

The tiger thought to himself:

"Just who is this fellow called 'Gotgam' that makes the crying stop so instantly? Gotgam must be a much scarier fellow than me!"

But just then, the mother opened the room door.

Startled, the tiger fled into the cow shed. While hiding there, the tiger saw someone creeping stealthily into the shed.

"That must be the Gotgam!" the tiger thought.

But in reality, the person entering the shed was a cattle thief who had come to steal a cow.

Not knowing this, the tiger trembled with fear. He crouched down next to the cow and hid.

Right then, the cattle thief, approaching slowly, felt around and touched the tiger's head.

The tiger was so startled that he jumped up. Because the tiger bolted upright, the surprised thief ended up jumping onto the tiger's back.

"Oh no, the Gotgam is on my back!"

Believing that the terrifying "Gotgam" was on his back, the tiger began to run as fast as he could.

The cattle thief also clung to the tiger with all his might so he wouldn't fall off.

The tiger thought:

"This is strange. Why won't the Gotgam fall off? He really is a terrifying fellow."

As he rode on the tiger's back, the thief thought it was strange that the "cow" was moving so fast.

"This is weird. Why is this cow so fast?"

Before long, day began to break. Only then did the thief realize he was riding a tiger.

"Good heavens, I'm on a tiger's back!"

The thief was terrified. Just then, he saw a large tree. The thief quickly jumped and grabbed onto a branch.

Then, the thief ran far away to escape the tiger.

"Whew, I almost got eaten by a tiger."

The tiger, realizing the Gotgam had finally fallen off his back, ran away even faster.

"Whew, I almost got eaten by the Gotgam."

It is said that after that day, neither the tiger nor the thief ever returned to the village again.

The tiger was too afraid of the dried persimmon, and the thief was too afraid of the tiger.
