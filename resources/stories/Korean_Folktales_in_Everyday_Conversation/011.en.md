Kongji and Patzzi

Once upon a time, in a certain village, a kind-hearted girl named Kongji lived with her father. Kongji's mother had passed away when Kongji was young. Then one day, as her father remarried, Kongji gained a stepmother. The stepmother had a daughter named Patzzi.

"Hello, Mother. Hello, Patzzi. Let's get along well from now on."

Kind Kongji gladly welcomed her stepmother and younger sister Patzzi. However, the stepmother and Patzzi were not happy to see Kongji. A short while later, Kongji's father passed away from an illness. After her father died, the stepmother only favored Patzzi and forced Kongji to do hard chores every day.

One day, the stepmother called Kongji and told her to fill a jar with water. "Hey, Kongji. Fill this jar with water to the top. If you don't finish by evening, you won't get any dinner tonight." "Yes, Mother." Kongji began to fill the jar with water. But no matter how much water she poured in, the jar would not fill up. "That's strange. Why isn't the water filling up?" Inspecting the jar, Kongji discovered a large hole at the bottom. The wicked stepmother had made her fill a jar that had a hole in it.

"Oh, there was a hole. How can I fill a jar that has a hole in it? I won't be able to eat dinner again today. Sob."

As Kongji was feeling upset, a toad appeared before her.

"Lady Kongji, do not worry. I will go inside the jar and block the hole. Hurry and fill the water."
"Thank you so much, toad."

Thanks to the toad, Kongji was able to completely fill the jar with water. And the stepmother had no choice but to give Kongji her dinner.

A few days later, a grand festival was held in the village. The stepmother and Patzzi were getting ready to go to the festival.

"Mother, how is this skirt? Does it look good?"
"It is truly beautiful. As expected, my daughter is the best."

Kongji was so envious of Patzzi that she said to her stepmother,
"Mother, I want to go to the festival too."

The stepmother thought for a moment and said,
"The festival? Alright, fine."
"Really?"

Kongji was overjoyed. However, the stepmother actually had no intention of taking Kongji to the festival.

"Instead, peel all the husks off this rice here, and weave all that hemp cloth over there before you come to the festival."
"What? All of that?"
"Or do you just want to stay home and work? If we all go to the festival, who will do the chores?"
"No, Mother. I will finish it all and then go to the festival."

The stepmother and Patzzi left Kongji behind and went to the festival first.

Kongji first started to peel the rice husks.

"Sigh, when will I ever finish all of this?"

Just then, some sparrows appeared and said,

"Chirp chirp, Lady Kongji, don't worry. We will help you."
"Thank you so much, sparrows."

Thanks to the sparrows, Kongji was able to peel all the rice husks. Next, Kongji began to weave the hemp cloth. But weaving hemp cloth took a very long time.

Kongji was worried that she wouldn't be able to make it to the festival. Just then, a fairy descended from the sky and said to Kongji.

"Kongji, I will weave the cloth, so you hurry up and get ready for the festival. Here are some pretty clothes and shoes."
"Thank you so much, fairy."

Having finished getting ready thanks to the fairy, Kongji was running to the festival.

Right then...

"The magistrate is passing! Everyone clear the way!"

Startled by the loud voice, Kongji tripped, and the shoe she was wearing fell into the river.

"Ah! Oh no!"

Hearing the noise and seeing Kongji, the magistrate fell in love with her at first sight. The magistrate tried to call out to Kongji. But the flustered Kongji had already disappeared. The magistrate pointed to Kongji's shoe floating in the river and told his servants

"You must find the owner of that shoe."

From the next day, the magistrate's servants went all around the village to find the owner of the shoe. However, they could not find the owner. "We've searched the entire village, but there is no one whom this shoe fits. Let's just go to that last house over there." That house was Kongji's house. When she heard the magistrate was looking for the owner of the shoe, the stepmother sent Kongji into the kitchen and told Patzzi to try the shoe on. However, the shoe did not fit Patzzi's foot. Then the servants said, "Could you also call the young lady who went into the kitchen earlier?" "That child wouldn't be the owner of this shoe. She doesn't own pretty shoes like this."

Ignoring the stepmother's words, the magistrate's servants called Kongji and had her try on the shoe. "Ahem, this is an order from the magistrate. Young lady, come here and try on this shoe at once." Kongji came out of the kitchen and slowly slipped her foot into the shoe. The shoe fit Kongji's foot perfectly. "We have finally found the owner of this shoe. Young lady, the magistrate is looking for the owner. Will you come with us?" Kongji smiled and answered, "I'd love to." The servants brought Kongji to the magistrate. The magistrate was truly delighted to meet Kongji again. "Before, you lost your shoe because you were startled by the sound of my passing, right? I am sorry. I saw you then and fell in love at first sight." Kongji also liked the magistrate who had found her shoe for her. And so Kongji and the magistrate fell in love, and it is said that the two lived happily ever after, getting married amidst the congratulations of many people.
