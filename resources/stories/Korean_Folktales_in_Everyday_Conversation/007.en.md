The Magpie That Repaid a Favor

Long ago, there was a man who was very skilled with a bow. One day, he was traveling to Hanyang to take the civil service examination. Because he lived in a village far away, he had to walk for several days to reach his destination.

As he was walking, a pair of magpies began flying over his head, crying out loudly. It seemed as though they were pleading for help. When the man looked toward their nest, he saw a massive snake slithering toward it. Inside the nest were baby magpies, trembling and crying in fear.

“Oh no, there’s a snake!”

The man quickly drew his bow and fired. The arrow struck the snake perfectly, and it fell to the ground, dead. The magpie parents circled over the man’s head for a long time, chirping as if to say thank you. Having saved the young birds, the man resumed his journey toward Hanyang.

As he continued walking, it began to grow dark.

“I’ll have to find a place to sleep for the night.”

Looking around, he spotted a light in the distance. As he approached the light, he found an old, secluded house.

“Is anyone home?” the man asked, knocking on the door.

“Who is it?” a woman replied, coming out of the house.

“I am on my way to Hanyang for the exams. I’m sorry to intrude, but could I stay here for the night? This is the only house in the area.”

“Of course, please come in.”

The woman provided him with a room and prepared a meal for him.

“Thank you so much. I apologize for arriving so late.”

“It’s no trouble at all. Please, rest comfortably.”

Exhausted from his long travels, the man fell into a deep sleep immediately after dinner. A short while later, he woke up suddenly, gasping for air. A giant snake was coiled tightly around his body. It was just as large as the snake he had killed earlier. The snake spoke to him:

“The snake you killed today was my husband. I will kill you tonight to avenge him.”

The woman had actually been the wife of the snake the man had killed, and she had transformed into a human to lure him in for revenge. The man pleaded for his life.

“I am truly sorry. But your husband was going to eat the baby magpies. I couldn't just watch and do nothing. Please, forgive me.”

The snake thought for a moment and then said:

“There is a temple at the top of that mountain with a large bell. If that bell rings three times before morning, I will let you live.”

“But that temple is abandoned! Who would go there in the middle of the night to ring a bell? Please, have mercy!”

The man wept, certain that he was going to die. But just then, the sound of a bell echoed through the air.

Dong—

Startled, both the man and the snake looked toward the mountain. The bell rang again.

Dong—

A moment later, it rang a third time: Dong—. Having heard the three rings, the snake had no choice but to release him.

“A promise is a promise, so I will let you go this time. But I will seek my revenge another day.”

When morning came, the man was curious about how the bell had rung, so he climbed to the temple at the summit. There, he found the pair of magpies lying dead beneath the bell. To repay the man for saving their babies, the magpies had struck the bell with their own bodies until they died.

“Thank you... you truly saved me.”

The man wept, overwhelmed with both gratitude and sorrow. Before continuing his journey to Hanyang, he carefully buried the magpies to honor their sacrifice.
