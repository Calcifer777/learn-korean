Han Seok-bong and His Mother

Once upon a time in a small village, there was a boy named Han Seok-bong.
Seok-bong's father passed away early, so Seok-bong lived alone with his mother.
Seok-bong’s mother made a living by making rice cakes and selling them at the market.
From a young age, Seok-bong was very interested in letters.

"Mother, how do you read this letter?"
"You read it as 'tree'."
"Then what about this one?"
"That is 'sky'."
"It's so much fun. The letters look just like pretty pictures."

While his mother worked at the market, Han Seok-bong played with friends who didn't go to the village school (seodang).
Seok-bong's friends liked playing outside every day, but Seok-bong grew increasingly bored of playing with them.

"Mother, I want to go to the village school too. And I want to learn letters."
His mother's heart ached.
Seok-bong's family was very poor, so he couldn't afford to attend the school.

"Why do you want to learn letters?"
"I want to learn letters so I can write beautifully. Like a painter."

Hearing Seok-bong's answer, his mother said this:
"I can't send you to the village school, but I will definitely buy you paper so our Seok-bong can practice writing."

Seok-bong was disappointed.
However, knowing how hard his mother worked, he didn't say anything more.
Seok-bong went to the village school every day to watch the other children studying.
And when he came home, he used a twig to trace the letters he had seen at the school.

A month later, it was Seok-bong's birthday.
His mother bought him paper and a brush.
"Seok-bong, you wanted this, right? Happy birthday."
"Wow, Mother! This is paper, isn't it? I'm so happy. Thank you."

Han Seok-bong was overjoyed just to have paper and a brush.
His mother was pleased to see Seok-bong so happy.
Seok-bong wrote letters on paper for the first time.
The feeling of writing on paper was so wonderful that he felt like he could fly.
The more he wrote, the more his desire to study grew.

"Mother, I like letters so much. I want to learn them properly."
"To learn properly, you have to go to Hanyang (the capital) and study letters for at least 10 years. Would you be okay with that?"
"Hanyang? Does that mean I have to live apart from you, Mother?"
"Of course, you must have that level of determination. To achieve great success with your handwriting, you must go to a big city. However..."

Han Seok-bong's mother was firm.
She was also very sad to part with her son, but she believed that sending him to Hanyang was for his own good.
So, Seok-bong left for Hanyang.
Han Seok-bong's mother worked hard selling rice cakes and sent money so Seok-bong could focus solely on his studies in Hanyang.
Seok-bong practiced his handwriting diligently for the sake of his hardworking mother.

After about 3 years, many people began to praise Han Seok-bong's handwriting.
"Look at this writing. It's really wonderful."
"Doesn't the content of the text look even better because of the handwriting?"
"You can say that again. Who on earth wrote this?"
"They say it's a young man named Han Seok-bong. It's truly amazing."

Han Seok-bong's name became increasingly famous, and Seok-bong even started earning money by writing for others.
Seok-bong thought he was finally ready to return home to his mother.
The next morning, Seok-bong left for his hometown.

It was night when he arrived.
"Mother, I'm here. Mother, I missed you."
"What? Is it Seok-bong? It's only been 3 years since you left, why have you come back already?"

His mother had deeply missed her son all this time too, but she held back her emotions and spoke firmly.
Han Seok-bong was flustered because his mother didn't welcome him.
"Uh, Mother, aren't you glad I came back early? Everyone is amazed by my handwriting skills. Do you know how famous my name is in Hanyang?"
"Is that so? Alright, come on in."

His mother sat Seok-bong down and prepared paper, a brush, rice cakes, and a knife.
Then, she blew out the lights in the room.

"Mother, why are you doing this? Why did you suddenly turn off the lights?"
Han Seok-bong was confused.

"I want to see this handwriting of yours that everyone praises."
"But with the lights off, I can't see anything?"
"If you really write that well, you should be able to write perfectly even in the dark. I will slice the rice cakes to sell at the market tomorrow, and you write."

His mother spoke firmly.
Han Seok-bong wasn't confident, but he wrote diligently.
10 minutes later, his mother turned the lights back on.

"Let's take a look."
When the lights were lit, Han Seok-bong's writing was crooked and messy.
However, the rice cakes his mother had sliced were all the exact same thickness and beautifully shaped.

"Do you call yourself a master calligrapher with handwriting like this? Hurry back to Hanyang."
His mother was truly heartbroken, but she knew she had to be strict for her son's sake.
Han Seok-bong was very sad, but looking at the messy writing he had done, he couldn't say a word.
Seok-bong returned to Hanyang shedding tears.

"I was too arrogant. I am still so lacking. I will work harder and definitely make my mother proud."
After returning to Hanyang, Seok-bong reduced his sleep, gave up playing, and dedicated himself entirely to his studies.
He practiced with all his might so he could write flawlessly in any situation.

After practicing for another 7 years, Seok-bong became the greatest calligrapher in Joseon.
Everyone praised Han Seok-bong's handwriting, and people from other countries even bought his writing for exorbitant prices.
"The writing is truly beautiful and majestic. He really is the ultimate master calligrapher, just as the rumors say."

Han Seok-bong finally returned to his hometown.
Seeing Seok-bong return, his mother was overjoyed.
She hugged Seok-bong tightly and said:
"Seok-bong, my son Seok-bong, I am so proud of you."
"Mother, you've worked so hard all this time. I will make you happy from now on."

The mother and son shed tears of joy as they embraced each other.
