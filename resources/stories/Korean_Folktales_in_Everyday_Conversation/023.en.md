The Rat That Ate Fingernail Clippings

Once upon a time, a couple lived in a small village. They had one son whom they loved dearly. However, the son did not listen to his parents and did not study hard. The couple grew very worried about him. "Look at our son. He does nothing but play all day. Isn't there some way to help him?" "How about sending him to a temple? Let's ask the monk to teach him strictly." "That is a good idea."

The couple called their son and said, "Son, we have decided to send you to a temple. Stay there for three years." "What? No! Why should I?" The son did not want to go. "Are you just going to keep playing and eating at home like this? Go and get ready right now!" Although their hearts ached, the couple forced their son to go to the temple. Upon arrival, the son saw the shabby temple and sighed, "Gosh, how can I live in a place like this?" He wanted to go home immediately.

A monk approached the sighing boy. "Welcome. That room over there is where you will stay. Go wash up first—and cut those long fingernails." After washing, the son trimmed his nails. As he was about to toss the clippings into the yard, the monk shouted, "You! You must not throw away fingernails carelessly. Something terrible might happen. Collect them properly before you go to sleep. Starting tomorrow, you must wake up early to work and study." The son replied in a small voice, "Yes..."

From the next day, the son stayed busy waking up early to work and study. Although it was difficult, he began to change little by little. One evening, while he was resting after his studies, a rat approached him, making "squeak-squeak" noises. The rat stared at him quietly. Finding it curious, the son gave the rat some of his leftover rice. After that, the rat visited him every evening. The son became comfortable with the rat and began sharing his thoughts. "I want to go home as soon as possible." Every time he spoke, the rat looked at him as if it understood everything.

The more he thought about going home, the less he wanted to work and study. He began to clean or study only when the monk was watching and stopped listening to the monk’s instructions. "My boy, didn't I tell you to organize and dispose of your nail clippings properly?" "I was going to do it in the evening." Seeing this, the monk sighed, realizing the boy had become lazy again.

Finally, three years passed, and it was time for the son to go home. "Wow! I finally get to go home tomorrow!" Excitedly, he packed his bags and took a bath. Lastly, he trimmed his fingernails. "Ugh, this is such a chore." He remembered the monk's warning not to throw them away carelessly, but because he was lazy, he just tossed them into the yard. "Eh, it's my last day, what does it matter?"

The next morning, the son ran home. "Mother, Father! I’m back!" But right then, someone said, "Who are you?" A boy who looked exactly like him was speaking to him. The son was shocked. "Me? I’m the son of this house! Who are you?" "What are you talking about? I am the son of this house!" "What? Don't lie! I am the real son!" The two boys eventually started fighting.

The parents ran outside, startled by the noise. "Goodness, what is going on?" The couple looked back and forth between the two boys. Their faces, voices, and even a small freckle on their bodies were identical. Eventually, the parents took the boys to the local governor. To find the real son, the governor asked, "Tell me, when are your parents' birthdays?" The real son was flustered and couldn't remember well. "My father's birthday is... March..." As the real son hesitated, the fake son quickly spoke up. "My father's birthday is August 5th, and my mother's is February 15th." The fake son answered much better than the real one. "You are the real son! Punish that impostor!" The real son was beaten and chased away, crying on the street.

"I am a bad son for not even remembering my parents' birthdays. Where should I go now?" Suddenly, the monk's face came to mind. The son headed back to the temple. He told the monk everything while crying. After listening, the monk gave him a solution. "Hide a cat and go back to the house. Pull the cat out in front of the impostor." The son found a cat nearby, hid it in his clothes, and went home.

"You again!" the parents shouted when they saw the real son. Hearing the noise, the fake son came out of the room and said, "Father, Mother! Kick that fake out immediately!" At that moment, the real son pulled the cat out from his clothes. The cat immediately pounced on the fake son. Then, something amazing happened. With a "poof" sound, the fake son turned into a rat! Seeing this, the parents rushed to the real son. "Oh my, you are our real son!" "Mother, Father!" The family hugged each other and cried.

A few days later, the family visited the monk. "Monk, thank you so much. But how did you know that bringing a cat would work?" The monk smiled and said, "Animals that live a long time can turn into a human if they eat that person's fingernails or toenails. This rat ate the clippings the boy threw in the yard and transformed. My boy, didn't I tell you to collect your clippings carefully?" The son deeply reflected on his actions. After that, he became a new person, studied hard, listened to his parents, and was always praised by the village.
