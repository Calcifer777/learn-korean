The Snail Bride

Once upon a time, there was a kind and hardworking man. One day, while working hard in the rice paddy, the man talked to himself.

"Phew, this is hard. Should I just go home? No, even if I go home, there is no one there, so it will just be lonely."

Just then, someone spoke in a small voice.

"Live with me."

Startled, the man looked around, but no one was there. He thought he had heard wrong and started working again. After finishing all his work, the man talked to himself again.

"Phew, what is the point of working so hard? There is not even anyone to eat this with."

"Eat with me."

The small voice was heard again. When the man looked around once more, there was a large snail.

"That's strange. There is nothing but a snail here."

That snail was much larger than any snail the man had ever seen until now. The man was amazed by the large snail and also felt lonely living by himself, so he took the snail home. Then, he put the snail he brought into a large water jar.

The next day, the man went to the rice paddy to work and returned home. Opening the door and coming inside, the man was very surprised. Delicious food was prepared, and the house was spotlessly cleaned.

"Wait, what is going on? There is no one to come to my house."

The man thought it was strange, but he deliciously ate the prepared food. The next day and the day after that, whenever he returned from finishing work, delicious food was always prepared. The man felt good, but he was also a little scared.

"Tomorrow, I must find out who has been coming by."

The next day, the man pretended to go to work and hid far away to watch the house. A little while later, a bright light shone from the jar where he kept the snail, and a woman came out of the jar. The man could not believe it.

"This can't be, a person coming out of a jar."

The woman who came out of the jar was truly beautiful. The man fell in love with her at first sight. The woman spotlessly cleaned the house and made delicious food. Then, she tried to go back into the jar. The man quickly ran over and said:

"Wait a moment."

The woman was startled to see the man.

"Who are you? Why are you cleaning and cooking for me?"

The woman answered the man's question.

"You put a snail in this jar a few days ago, right? I am that very snail. I am actually the daughter of the Dragon King of the Sea, but I was punished for not listening to my father and was turned into a snail. You heard my voice and brought me to this house. I was so grateful to you that I did the cleaning and cooking. I will become a human again in a few days. Thank you for everything."

The man was moved by the woman's warm words. And he thought that he wanted to live with someone so warm-hearted.

"I am truly grateful too. Thanks to you, I could eat delicious food in a clean house for the past few days. I want to be with someone as warm-hearted as you. Will you marry me?"

The woman did not dislike the man who spoke with all his sincerity. So, the woman and the man got married and spent happy days together.

Then one day, it happened. A magistrate famous for being wicked was passing by on horseback and fell in love with the woman at first sight. Seeing the man next to the woman, the magistrate asked the man:

"Who is this beautiful woman?"

"This woman is my wife."

"What? Such a beautiful woman is the wife of a poor man like you? It would be better for this woman to live with me. Let's make a bet; come to the mountain out back right away tomorrow."

The magistrate said that if the man won the bet, he would give him a large sum of money, and if the man lost, he would take his wife away. The man felt it was incredibly unfair, but he could not avoid the bet.

"What should I do? How could I possibly beat the magistrate? Sob, sob, sob."

Returning home, the man's tears would not stop. To such a man, the woman handed over the ring she was wearing on her hand and said:

"I will never part with you. I have a plan, so go to the sea right now, throw this ring in, and wait for a moment."

The man immediately ran to the sea and threw the ring. And an amazing thing happened. From inside the sea, the Dragon King's subjects appeared and took the man before the Dragon King.

"The magistrate is trying to take my wife away. Please help me."

The man told the Dragon King everything that had happened so far. Hearing the man's words, the Dragon King gave him a small bottle and told him to open it whenever he needed help.

The next day, the man took the bottle and went to the mountain out back.

"Let's make it so that the person who plants trees faster wins."

The magistrate brought many subjects and began to plant trees. The magistrate and his subjects were so fast that it seemed the man would lose. The man quickly opened the bottle the Dragon King had given him. Then, countless people came out of that bottle and started planting trees together with the man. Thanks to this, the man was able to win the bet easily.

However, the wicked magistrate did not give up.

"Nonsense. This time, let's bet on who rides a horse and runs faster."

The magistrate had a very large and good horse, but the man had no horse. The man opened the bottle the Dragon King had given him. Then, a small, skinny horse came out from inside it.

"Oh dear, can I beat the magistrate with such a small and skinny horse?"

Seeing the magistrate's large horse, the man became worried. But once the bet started, the small, skinny horse the man rode began to run much faster than the magistrate's large horse. In the end, the man beat the magistrate again.

"Let's make a bet one truly last time."

Angry at losing twice, the magistrate asked to bet again. This time, it became a bet where the person who goes further by boat wins. Compared to the magistrate's large and splendid boat, the man's boat was small and shabby. The magistrate's boat started going much faster than the man's boat.

The man opened the bottle this time as well. Then, a wondrous thing happened. Suddenly waves started crashing, and the waves overturned the magistrate's boat. Almost dying by falling into the sea, the magistrate now became terrified of making bets with the man.

"I have lost."

The magistrate gave up on taking the woman and gave the promised large sum of money to the man. Thanks to the bottle given by the Dragon King, the man who won the bets became rich just like that, and it is said he lived happily with his wife for a long, long time.
