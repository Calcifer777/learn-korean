The Old Man with a Lump

Once upon a time, there lived an old man with a large lump under his chin. The old man was kind-hearted and sang very well. One day, the kind old man went to the mountain to gather wood.

The old man gathered wood diligently. But suddenly, the sky grew dark and raindrops began to fall. The old man looked around to see if there was a place to rest. Then, he saw a house far away. Going closer, he saw it was a shabby, abandoned house. The old man thought he should sleep there for the night and went inside.

However, trying to sleep alone in an empty house was very scary. So, the old man began to sing.
"Moon, moon, bright moon."
"Moon, moon, bright moon."
Right at that moment...

"Bravo, bravo, wonderful!"
Someone praised the old man. The old man was so startled he looked behind him. Scary-looking goblins (dokkaebi) were looking at him.

"How can you sing so well? Tell us quickly!"
The goblins asked the old man with terrifying expressions. The old man was very scared, but he thought calmly.
'If I answer wrong, I might die. Pull yourself together. How should I answer to get out of here safely?'
And soon he spoke like this.

"Do you see this lump? Actually, this lump is a song pouch. The songs come out of this lump."
At the old man's words, one goblin said,
"Songs come out of this lump? Then give that lump to us. In return, we will give you treasure."

Without even listening to the old man's answer, the goblin tapped the old man's lump with his magical club. Then, the old man's lump fell right off. The goblin then tapped the floor with his club again. Suddenly, all kinds of treasures appeared.

Thus, the kind old man was able to return home having gotten rid of the lump he didn't like and having gained a mountain of treasure. The old man was truly happy.

A few days later, an old man with a lump from a neighboring village heard the story that the kind old man had gotten rid of his lump and gained treasure. The old man from the neighboring village was bad-hearted and greedy. The greedy old man was already rich, but he wanted to become even richer, so he went to see the kind old man. The kind old man told him everything that had happened on the mountain.

The next day, the greedy old man immediately went to the house where the goblins had appeared. When the dark night came, the greedy old man sang a song.
"Moon, moon, bright moon."
"Moon, moon, bright moon."

After a while, he heard the sound of the goblins coming from outside. The greedy old man was excited at the thought of becoming rich.
'Now I can get rid of my lump and become a very rich man too!'
As soon as the goblins entered the room, the greedy old man excitedly said,

"My singing voice is very beautiful, isn't it? Beautiful songs come out of this lump. Please take this lump!"
However, the goblins who entered the room got very angry at the greedy old man.
"What? Songs come out of a lump? You liar! The lump you gave us last time was fake too! Do you think we'll be fooled again?"

"No, it wasn't me who gave you the lump last time."
The flustered greedy old man pleaded, but the goblins did not listen to him.
"You rascal! Take back the lump you gave us last time too!"

The angry goblins attached another lump to the greedy old man's chin. The greedy old man returned to the village crying. By being greedy and trying to become richer, the greedy old man ended up with nothing but one more lump.
