Gyeonu and Jiknyeo (The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl)

A very long time ago, the King of the Heavens had a daughter named Jiknyeo. Jiknyeo was famous for her kind heart and her weaving skills. The fabric she wove shone brightly. Jiknyeo wove diligently every day.

"Look at the fabric Princess Jiknyeo wove. Doesn't it shine brightly?"
"How can Princess Jiknyeo be so kind and diligent?"
Everyone in the heavenly kingdom praised Jiknyeo.

Then one day, Jiknyeo briefly went outside the palace and met a young man herding cows. The young man's name was Gyeonu. Gyeonu was a farmer famous in the heavens for his diligence. From the moment they first saw each other, Gyeonu and Jiknyeo fell in love. Soon, the two went to see the King of the Heavens to get married.

"Father, I want to marry Gyeonu. Please give us your permission."
The King of the Heavens gladly permitted their marriage.
"Congratulations on your marriage, Princess Jiknyeo!"
"Congratulations, Gyeonu!"
The two were married amidst the congratulations of many people.

"Gyeonu, shall we go look at the flowers today?"
"Sounds good."
After getting married, Gyeonu and Jiknyeo did nothing but play and hang out every day. Hearing that the two were no longer working, the King called them in and scolded them.

"Please forgive us just this once. We will work hard from now on."
Gyeonu and Jiknyeo promised the King. However, the two soon broke their promise and started playing around again. Gyeonu continued to neglect the cows, and Jiknyeo did not weave. Eventually, the King called them in again.

"This simply will not do. Right this instant, Jiknyeo, go to the west of the Milky Way, and Gyeonu, go to the east of the Milky Way!"
The King of the Heavens punished Gyeonu and Jiknyeo.
"We were wrong. Please give us one more chance."
Gyeonu and Jiknyeo said with tears in their eyes. Seeing them like that, the King's heart softened.

"I will allow you to see each other once a year. On the seventh day of the seventh lunar month (Chilseok), you may meet across the Milky Way."
And so, Gyeonu and Jiknyeo were separated.
"Gyeonu!"
"Gyeonu..."
"Princess, you must take care until we meet again."
Gyeonu and Jiknyeo lived waiting only for the seventh day of the seventh lunar month. Gyeonu farmed diligently, and Jiknyeo wove diligently.

And finally, the seventh day of the seventh lunar month arrived. Gyeonu and Jiknyeo ran to the Milky Way. Standing right in front of the Milky Way, they searched for each other.
"Princess Jiknyeo, are you there?"
However, the Milky Way was so wide that Gyeonu and Jiknyeo could not see each other's faces. Unable to cross the Milky Way, the two could only cry and call out each other's names.
"Gyeonu!"
"Princess Jiknyeo..."

The tears shed by Gyeonu and Jiknyeo turned into rain and fell to the earth. Like this, every year when the seventh day of the seventh lunar month arrived, Gyeonu and Jiknyeo would run to the Milky Way from early morning. The two would call each other's names and cry profusely. Because of their tears, a lot of rain fell on the earth. The rain caused floods, and animals even lost their homes.

Because it flooded every seventh day of the seventh lunar month, the animals became very worried.
"What should we do if it floods every Chilseok? Isn't there a good solution?"
Just then, the crows and magpies flying in the sky spoke up.
"What if we make a bridge so that Gyeonu and Princess Jiknyeo can meet?"
"Make a bridge? How?"
"If we connect our bodies to make a bridge, the two can meet on the bridge. Then they won't cry, and there will be no floods."
"Wow, that's a great idea!"

The seventh day of the seventh lunar month returned. The crows and magpies flew to the Milky Way early in the morning.
"Come on, let's hurry and make a bridge."
"Sounds good, sounds good."
The crows and magpies connected their bodies to form a bridge.

"Gyeonu, Princess Jiknyeo! Hurry and step on us to cross!"
Gyeonu and Jiknyeo carefully walked across the bridge made by the crows and magpies. And finally, the two were able to meet.
"Gyeonu, I missed you so much."
"Princess Jiknyeo, me too."
The two embraced each other in joy.

Thanks to the bridge made by the crows and magpies, Gyeonu and Jiknyeo no longer cried, and there were no more floods. And it is said that from then on, if you look at the night sky every year on the seventh day of the seventh lunar month, you can see two very bright stars, which shine so brightly because Gyeonu and Jiknyeo are so happy to meet each other.
