The Lazy Man Who Became a Cow

Once upon a time, in a certain village, there lived a lazy man who hated to work. The lazy man’s wife found her husband, who slept all day long, pathetic. "Honey, you should go out and do some work too. We have to work to make a living." However, the lazy man did not listen to his wife. One day, the lazy man was eating and taking a nap as usual. "Honey, please wake up. How long are you going to just eat and sleep like this?"

Hating to hear his wife’s nagging, the lazy man left the house. As he wandered out, he saw a cow grazing in the shade and said, "That cow doesn't have to work and gets to rest comfortably. How great would it be if I became a cow too?" Just then, an old man who was passing by spoke to him. "If you want to become a cow, try putting on this mask. If you wear this mask, you can become a cow." "What? Really? You're saying I can become a cow if I wear this mask?" The lazy man quickly put on the mask. With a poof sound, the lazy man actually turned into a cow. "Wow, I really became a cow!" The lazy man was thrilled at the thought of doing nothing but eating and sleeping from now on. But suddenly, the old man who had given him the mask started dragging him away. "Come here, you cow. Let's go, quickly!"

"Huh? Old man? Where are we going? Wait a minute!" shouted the lazy man-turned-cow. However, the only sound that came out of his mouth was a "moo." Upon arriving at the market, the old man sold the cow to a farmer. Handing the cow over, the old man warned the farmer, "Never feed this cow a radish. If it eats a radish, it will die." "That's a very strange cow," the farmer replied. "No! I'm not a cow! I am a human!" The lazy man desperately tried to speak, but still, only "moo" sounds came out of his mouth. "Come here! Come on! Can't you move any faster?" From that day on, the lazy man-turned-cow was forced to work all day long in the farmer's field. "I'm being punished for living such a lazy life. Oh, honey, I'm so sorry." The lazy man deeply regretted his past days of playing around and avoiding work.

Then one day, while the lazy man-turned-cow was working exhaustingly in the fields, he spotted a radish patch. He remembered the old man's warning that he would die if he ate a radish. "Ah... I can't bear this hard life anymore. I would rather eat a radish and die." The lazy man pulled up a radish and ate it. Suddenly, a poof sound rang out, and a magical thing happened. The lazy man transformed back into his human form. As it turned out, eating a radish didn't kill him; it broke the spell and turned him back into a human. The old man had lied.

"Wow, I'm a human again!" The lazy man shouted, overjoyed to have his human body back. He immediately returned home and told his wife everything that had happened to him. And from that day forward, the lazy man changed his ways and became a hardworking man who worked harder than anyone else.
