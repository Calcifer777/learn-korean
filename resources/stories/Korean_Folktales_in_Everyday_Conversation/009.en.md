The Brother and Sister Who Became the Sun and the Moon

Once upon a time, in a deep mountain village, lived a mother and her two young children. The mother made a living by selling rice cakes. One day, after selling rice cakes in a neighboring village, she was heading home late at night.

"My children must be waiting; I must hurry home."

It was then that a huge, terrifying tiger appeared in front of her.

"Roar! If you give me a rice cake, I won't eat you!"

The mother was terrified. She quickly took out a rice cake and gave it to the tiger. The tiger ate it in one bite and said:

"Roar! Give me one more, and I really won't eat you!"

The mother had no more rice cakes left.

"I have no more rice cakes. My children are waiting for me at home. Please, spare my life!"

But the tiger ate the mother right then and there. After eating her, the tiger remembered her mention of the children at home. The tiger put on the mother’s clothes and headed toward the house where the siblings were.

The siblings were waiting anxiously for their mother to return.

"When will Mom be back?"
"She’ll be here soon."

Just then, someone knocked on the door.

"Children, Mom is home. Open the door."
"Mom!"

The younger sister started to open the door. However, the older brother felt that the voice sounded strange and stopped her.

"Wait, that’s not our mother’s voice."

The tiger was flustered.

"It is me. My voice is hoarse because I worked so hard. Hurry and open the door."
"Then please show us your hand."

At the brother's request, the tiger poked its hand through a gap in the door. Instead of their mother's soft, white hand, it was a rough hand covered in fur. Startled, the brother peeked through the gap.

"Oh my goodness, it’s a tiger! Let's run!"

Seeing the tiger outside, the siblings fled through the back door. They climbed up a tree in the backyard. The tiger, finally entering the room, realized the back door was open and went outside. It began searching for the children.

"Children, where are you?"

The siblings' reflection appeared in the well in the backyard. The tiger, thinking they were inside the well, tried to go in. Just then, a leaf fell from the tree into the well. The reflection disappeared. The tiger realized they weren't in the well.

"Roar! You weren't hiding in the well!"

The tiger spotted the siblings in the tree and began to climb up after them. The tiger got closer and closer. The siblings were terrified.

"Brother, we’re going to be caught!"

The brother closed his eyes tight and began to pray.

"Please, save us!"

Just before the tiger reached them, a rope miraculously came down from the sky. The siblings quickly grabbed onto the rope, and it pulled them up into the heavens.

Seeing this, the tiger also prayed to the sky.

"Please send a rope down for me, too!"

Another rope came down from the sky. The tiger quickly grabbed onto it. As the rope began to pull the tiger up, it grew excited at the thought of catching the children. But suddenly, the rope snapped.

"Aaaah!"

The tiger fell straight to the ground and died. The rope sent to the tiger was actually a rotten rope. The heavens had sent a strong rope to the kind siblings and a rotten one to the wicked tiger.

The siblings, holding onto the sturdy rope, reached the sky safely. After that, it is said that the sister became the sun and the brother became the moon, shining brightly over the world.
