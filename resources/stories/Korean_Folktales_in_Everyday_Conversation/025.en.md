The Story of Dangun

A very long time ago, there lived a king named Hwanin in the Kingdom of Heaven. The king had a son named Hwanung. His son Hwanung was more interested in the human world than the heavenly world. Watching the human world every day, Hwanung wanted to go down and see it for himself. Knowing his son's heart, the king said to Hwanung:

    "Go down to the human world and make that place more beautiful."

    "Really? Thank you, Father."

    "And take these heavenly objects: a mirror, a sword, and bells. The humans will know that you came down from heaven and will serve you."

Hwanung descended to the human world with 3,000 ministers and the heavenly objects his father had given him. Among the ministers were those who governed the wind, rain, and clouds. In this way, Hwanung began to rule the human world. Then one day, a bear and a tiger came to visit Hwanung.

    "Lord Hwanung, please help us."

    "What is the matter?"

    "We also want to become human. Please grant our wish."

    "Human? Becoming human is not an easy task."

    "We know that. We will do anything if only we can become human."

Moved by the earnest plea of the tiger and the bear, Hwanung gave them mugwort and garlic, and said:

    "To become human, you must stay out of the sunlight for 100 days and survive by eating only this mugwort and garlic. Can you do it?"

    "Yes, we can do it."

    "Thank you, Lord Hwanung."

From that day on, the bear and the tiger went into a pitch-black cave and began eating only mugwort and garlic. However, living like that every day was too difficult. The cave was cramped and dark, making the bear and the tiger feel terribly suffocated. Eating only bitter mugwort and spicy garlic was also extremely agonizing. After a few days had passed, the tiger angrily said to the bear:

    "Ugh, I can't eat mugwort and garlic anymore. I'm leaving right now."

The bear comforted the tiger and said:

    "Just a little longer, and we can become human too. Let's endure it together just a bit more."

    "Ugh, whatever. I'm just going to go out and eat some delicious meat."

With those words, the tiger ran out of the cave. Left alone, the bear was even more lonely and exhausted, but she patiently endured while waiting for the day she would become human. It was the 21st day since the bear had been eating only mugwort and garlic alone in the cave. Suddenly, a bright light shone inside the cave, and the bear transformed into a beautiful woman. The bear couldn't believe she had become a human when 100 days hadn't even passed yet. Just then, Hwanung appeared before the bear-turned-human. Hwanung was so proud of the bear for faithfully keeping her promise that he allowed her to transform into a human earlier than the promised time.

    "Oh my, I really have become a human. Thank you so much, Lord Hwanung."

The bear said, deeply moved. People called the bear who became a woman 'Ungnyeo'. Ungnyeo wanted to get married and have a child like other people. However, no one wanted to marry Ungnyeo, who used to be a bear. So, Ungnyeo prayed to heaven every day.

    "sob sob I also want to get married and have a child. Please grant my wish."

Whether it rained or snowed, Ungnyeo prayed earnestly. Seeing this, Hwanung was touched by Ungnyeo's sincere devotion. Therefore, Hwanung decided to grant Ungnyeo's wish once again. Hwanung transformed into a human man and married Ungnyeo. And, just as she had wished, Ungnyeo conceived a child. As time passed, the child was born with a very loud, resounding cry.

    "My baby, grow up to be great."

Later on, the child grew up splendidly as Ungnyeo had hoped, and founded a nation. That nation was Gojoseon, the very first state of Korea, and he was none other than Dangun Wanggeom.
