The Story of Hong Gildong

During the Joseon Dynasty, there lived a boy named Hong Gildong. Gildong was a special child. From a young age, he was smart and highly skilled in martial arts. Hong Gildong's father adored him for this.

Hong Gildong's father was a yangban (nobleman) holding a high-ranking government office. However, unlike his father, Gildong could not become a nobleman. This was because Gildong was the son of a slave who worked in his father's household. Because Gildong could not be recognized as a nobleman's son, he could not call his father "father," and no matter how good he was at martial arts, he could not obtain a government position.

One late night, Hong Gildong was practicing his martial arts. Seeing this, his father asked:

"Why are you out here doing this at such a late hour?"
"It is because I feel frustrated."
"Did something happen?"
"How can I not be frustrated when I cannot call my father 'father' and my older brother 'brother'?"

His father's heart ached deeply. However, fearing that comforting Gildong might make him arrogant, he deliberately spoke sternly:

"Do you not know well that it cannot be helped? Everyone lives this way. Do not say such useless things; come inside and go to sleep."

Hong Gildong knew his father's true feelings, but he was still very upset.

Then, one day. Gildong lay down to sleep when he heard a small noise outside his door. Having a bad feeling, Gildong quietly got up and stood by the door. A moment later, the door opened and someone entered the room holding a sword. Gildong quickly attacked the person and snatched the sword away.

"Who are you!"

As it turned out, the person was sent by another of his father's wives. She hated Hong Gildong because he was more outstanding than her own son, so she had tried to kill him.

The next morning, Gildong went to his mother and said:

"Mother, someone tried to kill me last night. I can no longer live in this house. You will only be safe if I leave."

Gildong's mother was very sad, but she could not hold onto him.

After leaving home, Gildong was walking through the mountains. From somewhere, he heard the voices of people. Following the sound, he found bandits gathered, choosing a leader.

"The person who lifts that boulder is our leader!"

Confident in his strength, Hong Gildong proudly stepped forward and said:

"I will also try to lift that boulder."

The bandits ignored Gildong, who looked young.

"Haha! This is no place for a little child like you. Hurry and go home."

It was then. Gildong lifted the boulder with ease.

"No, how can this be!"

Until then, no one had been able to lift that boulder. The bandits asked Hong Gildong to become their leader. To this, Gildong replied:

"Alright. However, we must become bandits who help people. The noblemen torment the people to become richer, while the poor become poorer. Let's punish these noblemen and return what they stole back to the people. And let's share it with poor people like us."

The bandits decided to listen to Gildong, who, despite his young age, was wise and highly capable.

"Yes, Boss!"

From then on, Hong Gildong and the bandits took money from those who became rich through bad means and distributed it to the poor. Gildong and the bandits started calling themselves Hwalbindang (the Party for Saving the Poor).

"We are Hwalbindang! It means to save the poor. Long live Hwalbindang!"

As time passed, Hwalbindang became famous, and more and more people started to follow Hong Gildong.

Soon, the King also found out about Hwalbindang.

"What is Hwalbindang?"

The King asked his ministers. The noblemen who had become rich through bad means hated Hwalbindang very much. So, they lied.

"Hwalbindang are very wicked bandits who steal money from good people. We must catch them right now."

Hearing the ministers' words, the King ordered:

"Catch Hwalbindang at once! Especially their leader, Hong Gildong, make sure to bring him here!"

When the ministers tried to catch Hong Gildong, he used magic to create seven fake Hong Gildongs that looked exactly like him. Because eight Hong Gildongs were seen here and there, the ministers could not catch him. The people, not knowing there were fake Hong Gildongs, saw him appear everywhere and said, "He flashes in the east and flashes in the west."

As time went by, the people started shouting not to arrest Hong Gildong, who had helped them. The King fell into deep thought.

'If he is such an outstanding person, rather than punishing him, I should make him work for the country.'

The King declared he would give Hong Gildong a government position instead of punishing him. A few days later, Hong Gildong came to the King on his own. He refused the position and said:

"I do not need a government position. Instead, please distribute rice and money for the poor people. If you do, I will quietly leave."

The King promised to do so.

Afterwards, Hong Gildong led Hwalbindang across the sea to an island called Yuldo. And it is said that they established a country called Yuldoguk there, where everyone lived happily and equally well, without noblemen or slaves.
