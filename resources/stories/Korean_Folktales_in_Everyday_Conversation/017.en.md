Kim Seon-dal Who Sold the Daedong River

Long ago, there lived a man with the title of Seon-dal who was famous for tricking others.
Because his surname was Kim, people called him 'Kim Seon-dal.'
One day, merchants from Hanyang (Seoul) came to the village where Kim Seon-dal lived.
The merchants were selling goods for twice the price they cost in Hanyang.

"Six coins? You can buy this for three coins in Hanyang!"

Hearing Kim Seon-dal's words, a merchant replied,
"The price of the goods is up to the seller. If you don't like it, go to Hanyang and buy it."

Kim Seon-dal had no choice but to hand over the six coins, but he found the Hanyang merchants detestable.
He wanted to teach these merchants, who were selling things so expensively, a lesson.
Then one day, Kim Seon-dal was walking by the Daedong River.
He noticed people diligently scooping water from the river.
His eyes sparkled.

"Ah, there's a way!"

That night, Kim Seon-dal called all the people who gathered water from the river to his house.
"I will give you coins now, so starting tomorrow, every time you scoop water, hand one coin back to me."

At his words, the people looked bewildered.
"Pardon? Why would we do such a thing?" one person asked.

"You will understand later. If anyone asks why you are paying, just tell them that I am the owner of the Daedong River."
"Well, it's not a difficult request, so we will do that."

The next day, Kim Seon-dal set up a desk and a chair in front of the Daedong River and sat down.
As agreed, the people handed him a coin every time they scooped water.
Just then, the merchants from Hanyang passed by the river.
They saw the people paying Kim Seon-dal for the water.

"Why are they paying that man after getting water?"
"I know, right?"

The merchants passed by, puzzled.
The following day, the people continued to pay Kim Seon-dal a coin each time they drew water.
A merchant, curious after witnessing the same scene as the day before, asked a passerby who had just paid.

"Why are you paying that man?"
"Because he is the owner of this river."
The person answered exactly as promised with Kim Seon-dal.

"What? The river has an owner?"
The merchants were extremely surprised.

"We travel all the way from Hanyang and work hard to earn money, but the owner of the Daedong River makes money so comfortably."
"They say the Daedong River never dries up all year round. I'm so jealous."

Listening to the others, one merchant suggested,
"How about we buy the Daedong River?"
"What? Buy the river?"
"It will probably be very expensive."
"If we pool our money together, we can do it. If we can just own the Daedong River, we will make much more money than we do now."
"Oh, that's a brilliant idea. Let's go meet the owner of the river tomorrow."

The Hanyang merchants decided to buy the Daedong River from Kim Seon-dal.
The next day, they approached Kim Seon-dal, who was sitting in front of the river.

"Hello, are you the owner of this river?"
"Yes, I am. What is the matter?"
"We are merchants from Hanyang. We want to buy this river."

At their words, Kim Seon-dal pretended to get very angry.
"This river is not something to be sold carelessly! I inherited it from my late parents!"

Seeing him angry, the merchants quickly said,
"Please wait. We brought a lot of money from Hanyang. We can give you whatever you want."
They believed that securing the Daedong River would be worth spending every penny they had.

"Whatever I want, you say?"
"Hmm. The Daedong River is very large and clean, and it never dries up. You know this, right? So how much would be fair?"

Kim Seon-dal acted as though he was pondering deeply.
"I should receive at least 100,000 coins so as not to disappoint my late parents."
"One... 100,000 coins?"

It was a much steeper price than the merchants had anticipated.
When they hesitated for a moment, Kim Seon-dal spoke up again.
"This is actually cheap. If you don't want it, forget it. There are many people who want to buy the river. Just yesterday, someone..."
"Alright. We will buy it. Here are 100,000 coins."

Anxious that Kim Seon-dal might sell the river to someone else, the merchants immediately handed over 100,000 coins and bought the Daedong River.
The very next day, the merchants set up a desk and chair by the river, just as Kim Seon-dal had done.
Right on time, people arrived to scoop water.
The merchants eagerly waited for them to pay.
However, the people walked right past them without paying.

"Excuse me, you have to pay if you scoop water. From now on, we are the owners of this river, not Kim Seon-dal."
The people looked at them in disbelief.

"What owner of the river? We were just returning the money we had received from Kim Seon-dal earlier."
The merchants finally realized they had been completely tricked.
By then, however, Kim Seon-dal had already fled and was nowhere to be found.
After that, it is said that Kim Seon-dal became famous as the swindler who made a fortune by selling the ownerless Daedong River.
