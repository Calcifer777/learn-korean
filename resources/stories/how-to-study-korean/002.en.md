A Day at the Zoo

I am Sohee, a seven-year-old whose absolute favorite animal is the penguin. I love penguins so much that I have five penguin plushies. Today was finally the day I got to go to the zoo with my dad to see penguins for the first time, so I was incredibly excited. We had planned to go to the zoo last week, but I suddenly caught a cold and couldn't make it. Mom made a lunchbox for Dad and me, and we are going to eat it for lunch. I left for the zoo with Dad at 9 o'clock, and Mom went to work. Of course, I wanted to go with Mom too, but she had to work anyway, so it couldn't be helped.

When we arrived at the zoo, the first thing I wanted to do with my dad was see the penguins. However, they said the penguin enclosure was far from the zoo entrance, so Dad and I decided to see the other animals first. The first animal we met was the elephant. The elephant was much bigger than what I had seen in books. But it wasn't scary; it was cute. The baby elephant was especially cute.

Sohee: "Baby elephant, how old are you?"
Dad: "Sohee, the baby elephant is still too young to speak. They say it's been two months since the baby elephant was born. Isn't it cute?"
Sohee: "Dad! It's so cute! I want to see the other animal friends quickly! Let's walk fast!"

After seeing the elephants, Dad and I walked quickly to see the giraffes.

'Giraffes are animals with long necks, but are their necks really as long as they are in pictures?' I asked myself this question. From a distance, I saw four giraffes slowly eating grass. Just as I had seen in the pictures, the giraffes' necks were incredibly long. Compared to my neck, they seemed ten times longer.

Sohee: "Dad! How can their necks be that long?"
Dad: "Haha, Dad doesn't really know either. Sohee, read a book later and let me know!"

Because of the giraffes' fur, it looked like they were wearing yellow clothes. I thought giraffes would be scary because of their long necks, but they were actually quite cute. I wanted to take lots of pictures and show Mom how long a giraffe's neck is when I got home. After seeing the giraffes, Dad suggested we eat lunch. Dad took out the lunchbox Mom had packed from his bag, and we enjoyed our meal. Ah! It was really delicious! After eating the sandwiches, I asked Dad to buy me ice cream.

Because I am the person Dad loves most in the world, of course he bought ice cream for me. After lunch, we went to see the lions. When Dad first talked about lions, he said they were strong, big, and scary, so I was really frightened. I was scared to see the lions, but we arrived at the lion enclosure anyway. However, the lion was taking a nap.

Sohee: "Dad! Lions sleep too? I thought lions didn't sleep!"
Dad: "Oh, our Sohee! Animals sleep too, of course! Shall we go see the penguins now?"
Sohee: "Dad! Let's hurry and see the penguins! I like penguins the most in the world! Let's go quickly!"

Dad and I walked quickly to where the penguins were. I walked so fast that I almost fell.
As we walked, I saw the penguins playing in the water from afar. The penguins were small and incredibly cute, just as I had imagined. I asked Dad to buy penguin food for me. That's because during the penguins' lunchtime, if you have food, you can feed them yourself. Five minutes later, Dad came back from a shop inside the zoo with penguin food. I happily accepted the food. I knew penguins ate fish, but I didn't know they ate such big fish. It was so fascinating that they could eat such big fish with their small mouths. I slowly approached to feed the penguins.

As I held the food, the penguins gathered around me.

Sohee: "Ah! So cute! I'll give it to you one by one! Penguins! Line up!"
Dad: "Haha! Sohee, penguins can't speak Korean. Our Sohee is so cute!"
Sohee: "Dad! Then what language do penguins speak? Penguin language? Why don't they speak Korean?"
Dad: "Penguins came from the South Pole, you know. And penguins talk with their bodies, not with words."
Sohee: "Amazing! Dad, I want to learn penguin language too!"

I couldn't believe it. The penguins I had seen in a book yesterday were right in front of my eyes. What's more, the penguins were eating the food I gave them. I wanted to touch a penguin, so I stroked its head while it was eating. It was a very soft and strange feeling.

After finishing with the penguins, Dad and I left the zoo. I wanted to stay longer, but my legs hurt too much and Dad looked a little tired as well. On the way home, I was hungry, so Dad and I ate pizza. While eating pizza, I kept talking to Dad about penguins. I think Dad thought it was cute that I kept talking about them, because he kept looking at me and smiling. After finishing the pizza, I bought a snack for Mom and we headed home. As soon as we arrived, I told Mom the sandwiches were delicious and showed her the pictures I took at the zoo. For 20 minutes, I explained to Mom all about the animals I saw today.

Sohee: "Mom! I saw lions, giraffes, and penguins today! You have no idea how cute they are! Next time, you absolutely have to go to the zoo with us! Promise me!"
Mom: "Alright. It sounds like you had a great time?"
Sohee: "Yeah! I had so much fun today! I want to go to the zoo again soon!"

I talked to Mom the longest about feeding the penguins. I also described how it felt when I touched the penguin. When I told Mom that I fed the penguins, she praised me for being brave.

After talking with Mom, I immediately washed up and lay down in bed. Then, I thought once again about everything that happened today. And I made a decision. In the future, I am going to be a zookeeper! That way, I will always be with animals! Just thinking about it made my heart flutter.
