The Leisure of a Cup of Coffee

These days, I have been under a lot of stress while adjusting to a new job. Since the workplace is far from home and I am starting a new career with new people, it has been difficult to settle in. However, because I have started doing work that I truly wanted to do, I am getting by well day by day. Today is a rare day where I can finally relax. I’ve been looking forward to this rest since yesterday. My plan was to wake up, do some light cleaning, go to the cafe in front of my house, and read a magazine while enjoying a sandwich and a cup of coffee. As it happens, the magazine I ordered two days ago arrived right on time.

As soon as I woke up, I started by cleaning my room. It was a chore I hadn't been able to do while busy adjusting to my new job for the past month, so it took longer than I expected. It’s true that you need to clean often—both for your health and for a better living environment! After cleaning, I grabbed my favorite magazine and headed out. I went to my favorite cafe and ordered a sweet cafe mocha and a sandwich. It feels like only a few days ago that I was sitting here writing my resume on my laptop, but it’s already been a month. Back then, every day was so tough, but now I’m so happy to be doing work that I enjoy.

Staff: "It’s been a while! Have you been very busy lately?"
Heeyoung: "Ah, I started working again a month ago. It feels good to be back after so long! Even though it’s morning, it’s quite crowded. It seems like business is still going well!"
Staff: "Haha, thank you. We have a new menu out this month, so make sure to try it later! It’s delicious!"

It’s always nice to come to a regular spot and have little chats like this. I’ll definitely have to try that new menu later. Ah, a cafe mocha is truly the most delicious when you’re feeling stressed.

That perfectly sweet yet bitter taste! It really is the ultimate coffee for me. Most people seem to drink Americanos, but I don’t think I’ll ever be able to drink those in my lifetime. A cafe mocha and a magazine make the perfect combination for me. Even just five years ago, I thought buying magazines was a waste of money, but these days, I can’t tell you how wonderful it is to read them. The only downside is that I immediately want to buy the new cosmetics featured in them.

It’s been such a long time since I sat down with coffee and a magazine. Everyone has their own way of relieving stress, and for me, it seems to be coffee and magazines. If my way of relieving stress had been shopping, it would have been very hard to save money. Thinking about it, I started reading magazines when I was in high school. I began to take an interest after accidentally reading a magazine a friend brought in. Since then, I’ve bought and read every new issue that came out each month. That habit has continued to this day.

After finishing my coffee, I reached out to a friend I hadn't talked to in a while. Before I got this job, it wasn't easy to contact friends. I wanted to reach out, but I lacked the confidence in myself. However, high school friends are naturally different. No matter what I do, they don't judge me and always welcome me with open arms. I decided that this was the perfect opportunity to treat them to something delicious.

As it turned out, the friend I contacted was nearby, so we decided to have lunch together. Lately, I’ve been so tired that it’s hard to walk around too much. I’ll be turning 30 soon. At first, I thought being thirty meant being "old," but now that I’m twenty-nine, I don't feel that way at all. A good thing about getting older is becoming more mature. I’m now able to better understand situations that I couldn't grasp before. After some thought, my friend finally arrived. As always, she was as bright as ever.

Heeyoung: "Sohui! It's been so long! How have you been? I'm sorry I haven't been in touch lately."
Sohui: "Hey, it's okay. Are we just casual friends? I can understand that much! Let’s go get something tasty!"
Heeyoung: "Yes! My treat! I just got my paycheck! What do you want to eat? It's so nice to see you after so long! You can pick something expensive!"
Sohui: "Really? Then let's have spaghetti and pizza. I’ve been eating Korean food every day lately, so since I’m with a friend, let’s have Italian! You like pizza, right?"
Heeyoung: "Of course! Who doesn't love pizza? I know a great place—let's go!"

We spent our time excitedly chatting and eating spaghetti and pizza. Talking with a friend made me remember many things from the past that I had temporarily forgotten. In high school, being together was so fun and going to school was a joy! It is still just as fun and happy to see her today. I didn't know it would be this great to still be friends with someone who shares the same memories. Sohui has been working at her current job for two years and told me she is getting a promotion soon. She worked hard at everything since high school, and she has reaped the rewards by working diligently at her job as well. Usually, one might feel jealous when friends have good things happen to them, but since I think of Sohui like a sister, I am genuinely happy for her promotion.

Since I haven't been at my job for very long yet, I don’t know when I’ll get promoted, but I believe that if I work hard day by day, I will see good results as well. Tomorrow, I will approach my work at the new office with an even more positive attitude.

After returning home, I took a shower and applied my favorite body lotion. It’s a lotion with a sweet scent that always makes me feel better. Tomorrow will be another busy day, but because I recharged today, I don't fear it! In fact, I actually feel like working hard! A few months ago, turning twenty-nine made me feel depressed, but I don't feel that way at all anymore. If anything, I have a good feeling that I’m going to become a confident woman who works hard at twenty-nine.
