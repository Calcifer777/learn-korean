My First Birthday Party

Today was my thirteenth birthday. My mom told me she was going to make special birthday food just for me. Since it was my very first birthday party, I even made my own birthday cards. There are six close friends in my class. I gave them the cards and asked them to come to my birthday. My friends promised me that they would come to my party. When school ended, I was incredibly happy. ‘When I get home, there will be delicious cake and food, right?’ I arrived home with an excited heart. Then, I counted to three. ‘One, two, three!’ I swung the door wide open. On a huge table, there was cake, snacks, and even pizza. I wanted to taste all the food right then and there, but I waited until my friends arrived.

Me: “Mom! I can’t believe it! You prepared this much food?”
Mom: “Of course, today is my daughter’s very first birthday party! Do you like it?”
Me: “Mom, I’m so happy! I want my friends to get here quickly so we can party! Is there anything I can do to help?”
Mom: “I’ve already done everything. Hurry and wash your hands, and get ready for the party.”
Me: “Okay! I love you, Mom!”

I quickly changed my clothes and prepared my favorite music. This was because I wanted to listen to music together while eating food after singing the "Happy Birthday" song with my friends. ‘Ah! My friends will love it so much when they come today, right?’ One minute felt like ten. After twenty minutes, someone rang the doorbell.

Me: “Who is it?”
Jiyoon: “It’s me! Jiyoon! Can you open the door quickly?”
Me: “Jiyoon! Thank you for coming to my birthday party! But when are the other friends coming?”
Jiyoon: “They’re all on their way together right now! I bought you a present! I want to give it to you right now, but I’ll give it to you together with everyone else!”

I was so happy! To be honest, I had been worried, thinking, ‘What if not a single person shows up?’ Since Jiyoon had arrived and she said the other friends were coming too, I was overjoyed. Five minutes later, all of my friends arrived at my birthday party.

Me: “Everyone! Thank you so much for coming to my birthday party! Eat lots of delicious food!”
Jiyoon: “Let’s sing the happy birthday song! One, two, three! Happy birthday to you~ Happy birthday to you! Happy birthday to dear Seulgi~”

I blew out the candles with a joyful heart! And I made a wish: ‘Please let me continue to live happily with my family and friends.’ One by one, my friends gave me presents. I received a variety of gifts, ranging from pencils and notebooks to stuffed animals. The gift I liked the most was a pink stuffed animal. It was so cute and the color was beautiful. We sang the birthday song, exchanged gifts, and ate delicious food. I felt great because my friends thoroughly enjoyed not only the cake but also the food my mom had made. Jiyoon especially loved the Japchae (stir-fried glass noodles) that my mom made. Jiyoon and I really have a lot in common; we both love Japchae so much.

After finishing the food with my friends, I danced excitedly to the music. I was shy at first, but after I started dancing, the other kids danced excitedly too. After dancing our hearts out, my friends went back to their respective homes. Once my friends left, my mom suddenly sang a song to me.

Mom: “Happy birthday to you~ Happy birthday to you~ Happy birthday to my dear daughter Seulgi!”

I couldn't stop crying. Mom gave me a small present. It was the exact watch I had wanted last year. Mom remembered what I had said last year. For ten minutes, I couldn't say a single word and just cried. I was so grateful to my mom, and I wanted to tell her that I loved her.

Me: “Mom, thank you so truly much. Today is the happiest day since I was born. Even though I’m not with Dad, I’m so happy because you love me so much. Mom, I will definitely become a great person in the future. And I’ll buy you lots of delicious things. I really love you.”

For a while, Mom didn't say anything and just looked into my eyes. She hugged me silently. I could see tears in my mom's eyes. I could tell—Mom and I were both thinking about Dad, who passed away five years ago. ‘On a birthday like today, if I could be with Dad, I would be even happier...’ But I was already so happy just with the fact that I could spend my birthday with my mom. Some kids might complain. They might say there aren't many presents, or that the food doesn't taste good. But I know. I know that just having family and friends to be together with makes me incredibly happy.

I helped my mom clean up. I tidied up the living room cleanly and expressed my heartfelt gratitude to her. Today was the happiest day of my life, the day I had my very first birthday party! Next year, I want to spend such precious time happily with my mom just like this!
