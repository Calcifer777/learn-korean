The 2026 Color is This Color

The mysteriously hued Transformative Teal has emerged as the Color of the Year for the 2026 S/S season. As a subtle color that seems to mix cyan, blue, and green, it is expected to establish itself as a trend color across fashion, beauty, and interior design this season.

A Color That Crosses Boundaries, Transformative Teal
'Transformative Teal' is the 2026 trend color co-presented by the British trend forecasting company WGSN and the global color research institute Coloro. Visualizing a mindset that respects the diversity of nature and prioritizes the Earth, WGSN stated that they hope this color evokes a sense of responsibility toward the environment and that more consumers will resonate with sustainable choices.

Transformative Teal, which is impressive starting from its name, also possesses a unique color spectrum that could not be easily defined previously. Fusing dark blue and aquamarine, this color freely crosses the boundaries of cyan, blue, and green, refusing to be defined as just a single color. When it catches the light, the cyan hue stands out, while in darker tones, it looks like deep blue or deep green, creating completely different impressions depending on the situation.

Although it is a color inspired by nature, in the 2026 S/S collections, it was interpreted in a more sophisticated and urban way than ever before. While there weren't many collections that implemented Transformative Teal exactly as is, various brands selected this color—which can look like cyan or read as deep blue—and expressed it with their own sensibilities. If you want to try this color in your daily life, adding a pop of color with a single item, like a hat from the Loewe collection or Hailey Bieber's styling, is an effective method. Adding a fresh change to a look without being excessive, Transformative Teal is expected to reveal its presence as the most sensational color this season.

One Color, Infinite Interpretations

The interesting point is that this color does not simply stop at being a "pretty color." Transformative Teal creates completely different impressions depending on the light, material, and styling, flexibly expanding the mood of a look. Thanks to its dual nature—sometimes looking like a calm navy, and momentarily reading as a refreshing green tone—it permeates broadly from daily wear to formal and resort looks. This subtle color that crosses the boundaries of seasons and genres becomes a sufficiently attractive option for those who want to broaden their style horizons with just a single color.
