# 봄날의 개 (The Cheerful Dog)

옛날 옛날, 감정을 아주 잘 숨기는 개가 한 마리 살았어요.
Once upon a time, there lived a dog that was very good at hiding its emotions.

개는 커다란 나무 그늘 아래 묶여 있었죠.
The dog was tied up under the shade of a large tree.

항상 꼬리를 살랑거리고 애교를 부려서 사람들은 그 개를 '봄날의 개'라고 불렀어요.
Because it always wagged its tail and acted cute, people called it 'the cheerful dog' (the dog of spring days).

낮에는 동네 아이들과 즐겁게 뛰어놀았지만,
During the day, it played happily with the neighborhood children,

밤이 되면 묶인 줄을 끊고 봄의 들판을 마음껏 달리고 싶어서 몰래몰래 끙끙 앓았어요.
but when night fell, it secretly whined and whimpered because it wanted to cut its leash and run freely in the spring fields.

그러던 어느 날, 마음속의 목소리가 개에게 물었어요.
Then one day, an inner voice asked the dog:

"얘, 넌 왜 줄을 끊고 도망가지 않니?"
"Hey, why don't you cut the leash and run away?"

그러자 봄날의 개가 대답했어요.
Then the cheerful dog replied:

"나는 묶여 있는 것에 너무 오래 길들여져서, 목줄 끊는 법을 잊어버렸어."
"I've been tied up for so long that I've forgotten how to break free."
