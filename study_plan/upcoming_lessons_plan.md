# Upcoming Lessons Plan (Derived from Story 16 Analysis)

## Lesson 022: Birthdays & Traditions (Current)

- **Focus:** Indirect Quotation (Statements) & Sequencing.
- **Grammar:**
  - **-다고 하다:** It is said that... (Adjectives/Verbs/Nouns).
  - **The Plain Form:** Skeleton sentence structure.
  - **-(으)ㄴ 후에:** After doing [Verb].
  - **Vocabulary:** 생일, 미역국, 낳다, 태어나다.

## Lesson 023: Suggestions & Emphasis (Next)

- **Source:** Story 16 (lines 4 & 5).
- **Grammar:**
  - **-(으)ㄹ까요?:** Shall we...? / I wonder...?
  - **-잖아요:** You know / As you know (Emphasis/Reasoning).
- **Context:** "Why do we eat soup?" / "Mom suffered, you know."

## Lesson 024: Causatives & Starting Actions (After Next)

- **Source:** Story 16 (lines 2 & 6).
- **Grammar:**
  - **-게 하다 / -게 해 주다:** To make [someone/something] [Adj/Verb].
  - **-기 시작하다:** To start [Verb-ing].
  - **-기:** Nominalization (Verb -> Noun).
- **Context:** "Seaweed makes blood clear." / "Started eating."
